# Landing Page Content & Guidance
## International Student Hours Tracking Application

### Purpose of the Landing Page
The landing page must clearly explain:
- What problem the app solves
- Who it is for (students & employers)
- How it works in practice
- Why it is legally safe and trustworthy
- What it does NOT claim to do

Tone:
- Professional
- Compliance-focused
- Clear and reassuring
- Not salesy or exaggerated

Avoid:
- Absolute legal claims
- “Guaranteed compliance” language
- Anything implying the platform is an authority

---

## Hero Section

### Headline
**Work Hour Compliance Made Simple for International Students & Employers**

### Subheading
Track, calculate, and record student work eligibility with confidence — built around student declarations and employer due diligence.

### Supporting Text
This platform helps international students and employers manage weekly working limits by combining student-declared academic status, automated calculations, and clear audit trails.

The system supports compliance — it does not replace legal responsibility.

### Primary CTAs
- “Get Started as a Student”
- “Employer / Manager Access”

---

## How It Works (Core Section)

### Student Side
1. Student logs in
2. Student declares:
   - University
   - Course start & end dates
   - Term and holiday dates
3. Optional upload:
   - Right-to-work share code
4. The system calculates:
   - Term-time vs vacation rules
   - Maximum allowed weekly hours
5. Student confirms their information regularly

**Key point:**  
The student remains legally responsible for the accuracy of their declarations.

---

### Employer / Manager Side
1. Manager searches by:
   - Student email or generated ID
2. Manager can view:
   - Student-declared eligibility
   - Calculated weekly hour limits
   - Confirmation timestamps
   - Historical records
3. Student confirms worked hours weekly

This gives employers documented proof of due diligence.

---

## Why Student Confirmation Is Required (Very Important)

### Clear Explanation
The platform does not determine or guarantee legal work rights.

Only the student can confirm:
- Their current academic status
- Whether term dates are accurate
- Whether any changes have occurred

By confirming regularly, the student:
- Acknowledges responsibility
- Creates a dated record
- Protects both themselves and the employer

### Short Statement (UI-friendly)
“Eligibility is calculated based on student-declared information and confirmed by the student. Employers rely on these confirmations as part of their compliance process.”

---

## What This Platform Is (And Is Not)

### What It IS
- A compliance support tool
- A calculation and record-keeping system
- An audit trail for employers
- A transparency tool for students

### What It Is NOT
- A legal authority
- A visa decision-maker
- A guarantee of compliance
- A replacement for employer checks

This distinction must be visible on the landing page.

---

## Key Benefits Section

### For Students
- Avoid accidental visa breaches
- Understand weekly work limits clearly
- Keep a personal work history
- Share eligibility information with employers easily

### For Employers
- Documented due diligence
- Clear eligibility snapshots
- Weekly confirmation records
- Reduced compliance risk

---

## Trust & Compliance Language

Use wording like:
- “Based on student-declared information”
- “Confirmed by the student on [date]”
- “Supports compliance processes”
- “Designed for record-keeping and transparency”

Avoid:
- “100% compliant”
- “Legally guaranteed”
- “Official verification”

---

## Footer Disclaimer (Required)

Suggested wording:
> This platform supports compliance by recording student declarations and confirmations. It does not provide legal advice or determine immigration status. Responsibility remains with the student and employer.

---

## Visual & UX Guidance
- Clean, calm design
- Use icons sparingly
- Avoid flashy marketing claims
- Prefer clarity over hype
- Emphasize trust and responsibility

---

## Overall Goal
A visitor should leave the landing page thinking:
“This doesn’t promise impossible things — it helps me prove I did the right thing.”
